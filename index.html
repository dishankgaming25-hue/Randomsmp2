A few simple rules help make Random SMP a great place to play.

  1. 01

    BE RESPECTFUL

    Treat players and staff with kindness. Harassment and hate speech aren’t welcome.

  2. 02

    PLAY FAIR

    No cheating, hacked clients, exploits, or anything that gives an unfair advantage.

    KEEP CHAT FRIENDLY

    Follow staff instructions and use the Discord to report problems or ask questions.

GOT QUESTIONS?

QUICK ANSWERS.

How do I join the server?+

Open Minecraft, add a multiplayer server, and enter Randomsmp.softhost.pro. Bedrock players should also enter port 19132.

Can Java and Bedrock players join?+

Yes. Java players use the server address shown above. Bedrock players use the same address with port 19132.

Are ranks required to play?+

No. Ranks are optional examples; you can join and enjoy the server without purchasing anything.

Where can I get help or meet the community?+

Join our Discord community to ask questions and connect with other players.
